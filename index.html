How is my score calculated? Four categories, each out of 100

Transactions: 3,000 outgoing transactions = 100 points (30% weight). Wallet age: activity since Base Mainnet's August 9, 2023 public launch = 100 points (25%). Contract destinations: 50 different destinations = 100 (25%). Recent activity: 20 active days in the last 30 = 100 (20%). The overall score is the weighted average, also out of 100.

Independent community scoring, not official Base rewards or airdrop eligibility. Data from sampled wallet history can be incomplete.
